LAUNDRY · SMALL HOME GUIDE

7 Space-Saving Organizers for Small Laundry Rooms

Laundry storage has to handle the actual routine: sorting, washing, drying and putting things away. Keep detergent accessible, protect ventilation and leave enough room to open the washer and dryer fully.

These are practical storage ideas and shopping criteria, not hands-on product reviews. Product links will be added after specific options are selected.
01

Stackable bins

Use separate bins for stain treatment, dryer supplies and spare cleaning cloths. Stable stacking saves shelf space, while a front opening keeps the lower bin reachable.

PRODUCT TO CONSIDER

Stackable laundry storage bins

Measure shelf depth and keep chemical products secured according to label directions.

02

Slim rolling carts

A cart can hold detergent and small supplies in the gap beside a machine. Leave space for hoses, vents and service access; never force a cart into a gap that needs airflow.

PRODUCT TO CONSIDER

Narrow rolling laundry cart

Measure the entire gap, including baseboards, hoses, cords and wheel clearance.

03

Wall shelves

A shelf above machines can store baskets and folded towels if it is securely installed. Keep heavy containers where they can be lifted safely, and leave appliance and vent clearance.

PRODUCT TO CONSIDER

Wall-mounted laundry storage shelf

Check wall type, mounting hardware, load rating and appliance access.

04

Wall hooks

A few hooks can give mesh bags and cleaning brushes a place to dry. Keep hanging items away from appliance doors and the path through a narrow laundry room.

PRODUCT TO CONSIDER

Wall or over-door utility hooks

Choose the mounting style for the surface and check clearance and load limits.

05

Under-shelf baskets

A shallow basket below an existing shelf can collect dryer sheets or cloths without adding another shelf. Confirm the clips fit the shelf thickness and the basket will not interfere with machine lids.

PRODUCT TO CONSIDER

Slide-on under-shelf storage basket

Measure shelf thickness, depth below it and the basket's rated load.

06

Labeled containers

Labels help you find supplies, but keep detergent and other chemicals in their original, labeled packaging. Use labeled bins to group those containers instead of decanting them.

PRODUCT TO CONSIDER

Handled labeled storage bins

Check bin dimensions and keep hazardous products out of children's reach.

07

Foldable drying racks

A rack that folds flat provides air-drying space only when needed. Put it where laundry can dry without blocking the washer, a doorway or a heat source.

PRODUCT TO CONSIDER

Foldable freestanding drying rack

Check open footprint, folded storage size and garment weight capacity.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Measure first. Organize second.

Check width, depth, height, door clearance and product instructions before ordering. Buy for a specific storage problem, and leave the room easy to use and clean.
