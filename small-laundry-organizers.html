7 Space-Saving Organizers for Small Laundry Rooms

Whether your washer and dryer sit in a closet, a bathroom corner or a small utility room, storage should support the wash-day routine: sort clothes, pretreat stains, wash, air-dry what needs it and put supplies away. Leave doors, vents and service access clear.

Stackable bins

Use front-access drawers for clean washcloths, mesh bags and spare dryer sheets. You can reach the lower level without unstacking everything. Keep liquid detergent and laundry packets out of these open, easy-access drawers, especially where children may be present.
